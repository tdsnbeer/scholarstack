About ScholarStack

ScholarStack is a small tracker for students who know scholarship money exists but rarely finish the applications. It shows a short list of scholarships ordered by deadline, lets the student mark each one (started, essay drafted, submitted or skipped, with a one-tap reason), puts deadline reminders and a weekly check-in into their own calendar, and shows which applications can share an essay.

Privacy. This site holds the app and, at most, a student's list in locked (encrypted) form. The key that opens it is made on their family's own computer and reaches the phone through a QR code, in the part of the link after the #, which browsers never send to any website. Progress is saved only in the phone's browser. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking and no messages.
